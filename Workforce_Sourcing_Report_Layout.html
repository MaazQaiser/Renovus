The answer

Base case · 408 employees · $42.9M fully-burdened annual labour cost
We assessed all 408 roles across the portco's 25 departments against five outsourceability dimensions. About 160 roles, carrying $15.5M of burdened cost, can credibly move to a sourcing partner — concentrated in IT applications, infrastructure operations, learning content production and marketing production. At the deal team's 50–60% offshore cost assumption that is a run-rate saving of $6.76M a year, or 15.8% of total labour cost, with roughly $4.5M of one-off transition cost and payback inside 14 months. We would start with the learning-content and development layers, where the work is codified and no student data is touched. The binding constraint is FERPA: it holds the 113-person enrollment organisation onshore and caps the support centre, so the largest headcount pool is not the largest opportunity.

Risks & next steps

What breaks this, and the first 30 days

Principal risks

FERPA exposure · High

Any offshore access to student records is a contract and accreditation event. Mitigation: data-residency controls and FERPA-gated ticket tiers audited before Wave 3, not during it.

Client US-staffing clauses · High

Contracts naming onshore individuals or requiring US-based staff could void part of the IT Applications case. Mitigation: contract review in the first 30 days, before any partner commitment.

Knowledge concentration · Medium

Several application-architect and SIS roles are single points of failure. Mitigation: 2-month overlap is the floor, not the target, for those specific roles; retention bonuses where needed.

Utilisation data gaps · Medium

40% of rows have no time-tracking, so billable-hour dilution from a move is unquantified. Mitigation: 90 days of tracked data before Wave 2 scope is fixed.

Detail

Assumptions register, method and data quality — everything needed to check the numbers
Cost assumptions are Renovus deal-team assumptions, not vendor pricing. No quote is in hand. Offshore cost is modelled at 50–60% of onshore fully-burdened cost, varied by function: IT Applications & Development 60%, IT Infrastructure & Ops 55%, Marketing & Creative 55%, Learning & Content 55%, Client-Facing & Corporate 55%, Support Center 50%, Enrollment & Admissions 50%.

Data tier and source

Tier A — row-per-person payroll export with 2023 base pay, bonus potential, benefits and fully-burdened cost, plus department, job title, core skill set, supervisor and a client-billable / Collegis-effective / non-billable time split. 409 rows received; one row dropped for having no title, skill set or pay, giving 408 roles and $42.9M of fully-burdened cost. Tier A supports full depth: per-role scoring, loaded-cost deltas, transition one-offs, ramp and three-year cumulative.

Scoring method

Five dimensions, each 1–5, higher meaning more outsourceable: codifiability of the work (30% weight), inverse of customer and stakeholder exposure (20%), inverse of IP, data and regulatory sensitivity (20%), inverse of seniority and institutional knowledge (15%), and offshore or nearshore talent availability (15%). The weighted result is expressed 0–100. High is 70 or above, Medium 50–69, Low below 50 and retained. Function scores shown on tab 3 are cost-weighted averages of the underlying role scores, not simple means.

Rule-based exclusions applied before scoring: every director-plus and VP role, every intern, and every role whose work requires physical presence at a client campus. These roles cannot appear in a wave regardless of their arithmetic score. Where a leader's team moves but the leader does not, that is recorded as a hybrid-model note rather than a score change.

Constraints applied as hard ceilings

FERPA and student PII. Confirmed by the deal team. Caps Enrollment & Admissions at Low and holds Support Center at Medium with tier-gating; also restricts the SIS administration roles inside IT Applications. Roughly $6.7M of otherwise-addressable cost is held onshore by this constraint alone.

Client contracts requiring US-based staff. Confirmed by the deal team. Applied as a 15% onshore retention overlay on IT Applications & Development and a 20% overlay on Marketing & Creative, pending the contract review named in the 30-day actions. If the review finds broader clauses, the IT Applications saving of $2.06M is the figure most at risk.

Transition assumptions — light profile

Two-month knowledge-transfer overlap, costed as 2/12 of the annual burdened cost of every moved role ($2.58M). Four weeks severance, costed at 4/52 of burdened cost ($1.19M). Partner standup and offshore recruitment at 5% of addressable cost ($0.77M). Six-month ramp to full run-rate within each wave, which is why Year 1 realises 45% of the run-rate and lands at a net outflow of $1.50M before turning positive in Year 2.

Data quality items

One record dropped entirely for missing title, skill set and all pay fields (330 Support Center). The Client Billable column is empty for every row in the file and was ignored. Core Skill Set is blank across all of 330 Support Center and most corporate departments, so scoring there leaned on job title alone and carries more uncertainty than elsewhere. Utilisation is recorded as "n/a" for roughly 40% of rows — leadership, interns, all of 445 ASU Enrollment, 330 Support Center and the corporate functions — and was treated as not tracked rather than as 100% non-billable, per the deal team's instruction. Two departments contain a single skill-set value of "0" or "NO MERIT" which were read as missing. Bonus potential is stated as potential, not paid, and is included in burdened cost at face value; if actual payout runs below potential the cost base and therefore the saving are both slightly overstated.

What is not modelled

No EBITDA uplift and no implied enterprise value at exit. The deal team did not supply current EBITDA, revenue or the deal model's exit multiple, and this assessment does not invent them. If those figures are provided, the base-case $6.76M run-rate saving flows to EBITDA at close to full value less any retained management overhead, and capitalising it at the deal model's multiple would give an illustrative EV effect — illustrative only, never a valuation. Also outside scope: severance tax and benefit-continuation detail, real-estate savings from reduced onshore footprint, revenue risk from any billable-hour dilution, and FX exposure on the offshore contract.

Reconciliation

Total burdened cost $42.93M across 408 roles. Addressable cost $15.46M across approximately 160 FTE. Base-case saving by function sums to $6.76M: IT Applications $2.06M, IT Infrastructure $1.82M, Marketing & Creative $0.95M, Learning & Content $0.85M, Client-Facing & Corporate $0.44M, Support Center $0.33M, Enrollment & Admissions $0.31M. Waves sum to the same total: Wave 1 $2.05M, Wave 2 $2.68M, Wave 3 $2.03M. Every High and Medium role in a non-retained function appears in exactly one wave; no Low-band, leadership or intern role appears in any wave. Individual-level data is held anonymised in the working file and does not appear in this document.
