$4.6M a year is addressable across 10 functions at Collegis Education

We assessed all 406 roles and $43.3M of loaded payroll at Collegis against a five-part sourcing rubric. 227 roles — 56% of the workforce, $18.1M of cost — are addressable, led by the IT and Salesforce delivery layer, the remote Support Center, and enrollment back-office. At offshore rates of 55–60% of onshore cost, the base case saves $4.6M a year and $9.2M net over three years, repaying transition costs in about eight months. We would lift out the 23-person Support Center first, then move the IT delivery layer. The caveat: Enrollment, the largest function, is accreditation-capped, so most of its saving requires a hybrid model.

Detail

Everything behind the numbers

D1 · Approach & scope

Approach and scope

A row-per-person payroll export (Tier A) covering 406 employees across 25 departments, with 2023 base pay, bonus potential, benefits, and a fully-burdened cost per person, plus reporting lines and client-billable time shares. The 25 departments were consolidated into 12 functions confirmed with the deal team; fully-burdened cost was used directly, so no loaded-cost multiplier was applied.

Eight summer interns and two nominal-cost strategic advisors are excluded from the sourcing model but remain in org totals. One Support Center row with no title or pay data is excluded pending data.

Every role was scored 1–5 on five weighted dimensions and banded High (≥70), Medium (50–69), or Low. Savings apply the deal team's offshore-cost assumptions to fully-burdened cost, net of a 15% recruitment fee, two months of overlap, and 60% year-1 realization.

Scoring dimensions

  • Codifiability (30%) — how routine, documented and measurable the work is.
  • Customer/stakeholder exposure (20%, inverse) — externally-facing roles score lower.
  • IP / data / regulatory sensitivity (20%, inverse) — regulated or core-IP access scores lower.
  • Seniority & institutional knowledge (15%, inverse) — tacit-knowledge roles score lower.
  • Offshore/nearshore talent availability (15%) — can the market actually staff it.
D2 · Organization

Organization snapshot

  • Enrollment & Admissions is the largest function at 110 FTE (27% of headcount) but only 18% of cost — a high-volume, mid-$50K specialist and coach population.
  • The technology estate is broad: five IT-related functions total 167 FTE and $17.9M, spanning Salesforce, Banner, Cvue, Jenzabar, LMS platforms and campus networks.
  • Leadership density is high: 63 roles at Leadership level, including a 27-person Partner Solutions & PM function that is almost entirely director-and-above client relationship staff.
  • Client-billable shares of 70–90% across the IT and SIS delivery teams mean workforce moves interact directly with client contracts, not just internal cost.
  • Compensation is mid-market: median fully-burdened cost is about $87K, so savings depend on volume of roles moved rather than a few expensive ones.

D3 · Potential

Outsourcing potential

High scores concentrate where work is queue-driven, remotely delivered today, and served by deep offshore talent markets: L1/L2 support in the Support Center, Salesforce and SIS administration, application and systems administration, network operations, data engineering, and digital course production in Learning Technology. These roles have measurable output, documented platforms, and no student-facing or campus-bound duties, and 28 of the 44 IT Infrastructure & Cloud roles clear the High bar on seed scores alone.

Low scores cluster around three things. First, physical presence: 31 ITSS field-support roles work on client campuses under contracts that require onshore staff, and video production and facilities roles need studios and buildings. Second, relationships: Partner Solutions, partner managers, and business development own institution relationships that do not transfer. Third, regulated judgment: instructional designers work directly with client faculty, and financial control, legal, and HR leadership carry sign-off duties that stay onshore.

Constraints applied

  • FERPA / regulated student and financial data caps SIS/IT Applications, Enrollment & Admissions, Support Center and Learning Technology — capped, not excluded, contingent on vendor data-processing agreements (Renovus deal team assumption).
  • Client contracts and physical presence pin ITSS field support (on-campus deskside/AV) onshore; the Support Center is capped rather than excluded.
  • Accreditation / state-authorization exposure caps student-facing Enrollment & Admissions roles at Medium; any move runs through a hybrid model with onshore compliance oversight.
  • Summer interns (8) and nominal-cost strategic advisors (2) are excluded from the sourcing model.

D5 · Savings

Savings model

For every role, the saving is its fully-burdened cost minus the assumed offshore cost for its function (55–60% of onshore, a Renovus deal-team assumption, not vendor pricing), counted at 100% for High roles and 35% for Medium roles in the base case. One-off transition costs add a 15% recruitment fee on offshore salaries plus two months of onshore overlap per moved role; year 1 realizes 60% of the run rate to reflect staggered waves.

D8 · Implementation

Implementation model and next steps

One sourcing partner, three constructs: a managed-service lift-out for the Support Center with SLA-based pricing; dedicated build-and-run pods for IT Infrastructure, IT Applications & SIS, Analytics and Learning Technology under retained onshore leads; and role-by-role staff augmentation for the smaller moves in Creative, Digital Marketing, finance operations and enrollment back-office. Retained functions (ITSS, Instructional Design, Partner Solutions) are out of scope for the vendor entirely.

No vendor is named; rates used are Renovus deal-team assumptions. A two-to-three-bidder RFP against the Wave 1 scope should convert them into firm pricing within six weeks.

Governance

  • Monthly steering: portco COO/CFO sponsor, Renovus operating partner, vendor delivery lead
  • Weekly delivery stand-up per pod during transition; escalation path from pod lead to steering inside five business days
  • Renovus IC checkpoint at the end of each wave against run-rate and service-quality gates

SLAs and KPIs

  • Support Center: first-response and resolution SLAs held within 5% of pre-transition baseline by day 90
  • IT pods: ticket backlog, change-failure rate, and client-escalation count tracked against the trailing-6-month baseline
  • Program: run-rate saving realized vs. plan, transition spend vs. the $2.8M budget, regretted attrition among retained staff
  • Compliance: zero FERPA incidents; DPA coverage confirmed before any system access is granted

Knowledge transfer

Each moved role follows a document-shadow-reverse-shadow sequence inside the two-month overlap, with the vendor producing runbooks as a paid deliverable. For the named-senior systems (Banner, Cvue, Jenzabar), overlap extends case-by-case and the retained onshore lead signs off on readiness before any onshore exit — the overlap cost is in the model; the sign-off discipline is what protects it.
